### The idea

Dental AI is a computer vision project exploring how machine learning can assist with the analysis of dental X-rays. The goal is to detect teeth and identify potential dental conditions, then present the predictions in a clear, visual interface.

### What I'm working on

- 🦷 **Tooth detection** — identifying teeth in dental X-ray images.
- 🔬 **Condition detection** — detecting target classes including impacted teeth, caries, periapical lesions, and deep caries.
- 🗂️ **Dataset preparation** — organising annotated datasets and preprocessing images for training.
- ⚙️ **Model training** — training YOLO11m with PyTorch and Ultralytics, including checkpointing and early stopping.
- 🎯 **Prediction visualisation** — displaying annotated X-rays and confidence scores.
- 🔗 **Tooth–condition association** — exploring how detected conditions relate to individual teeth.

### What's next?

- Improve disease detection and generalisation.
- Evaluate performance across different images and conditions.
- Refine tooth–condition association.
- Continue developing the application and its reporting workflow.
- Explore broader clinical decision-support capabilities as future work.

> **Important:** Dental AI is a research prototype, not a clinically validated diagnostic system. Its predictions should not replace assessment by a qualified dental professional.
